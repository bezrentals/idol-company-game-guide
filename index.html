Girls — Battle Stats

Stats from the Lineup Calculator (FILTER ONLY — Do NOT sort). Includes Skill DMG, Normal DMG, Reduce Skill/Normal, Fan Cap, Rally Cap, DMG Attack, and DMG Defense. Data converted: Damage to Player = Skill DMG + Normal DMG combined.

FILTER ONLY — Do NOT sort. Damage to player has been converted to equal Skill Damage and Normal Damage. VIP bonuses are NOT included here — see the Scorecard section below.
Reading the stats: Skill DMG — boosts active skill hits (higher = harder hits) Normal DMG — boosts basic auto-attacks Reduce Skill/Normal — cuts incoming damage (defensive) Fan/Rally Cap — increases max fans or rally size DMG Attack/Def — flat DPS added on attack or defense

Cards only show stats the girl actually has — blank means 0. Stats shown at Level 140 + triple gold skills.

CEO Event — Planner & Calculators

Three tools in one: Score Estimator (enter your inventory → see projected score), Goal Planner (set a target → see what you need), and Day Efficiency (which resources give most points per unit). Switch between event types below.

How to use: Pick your event type above, then choose a tool tab. Score Estimator — enter what you have → see your projected score. Goal Planner — enter a target score → see what you need to stockpile. Day Efficiency — see which resources give the most points per unit so you know where to spend first.
10–18% efficiency loss is sourced directly from the original community spreadsheet (April 2026). It reflects real observed conversion rates — not a guess. Use 10% as your optimistic estimate and 18% as your conservative floor when planning.
