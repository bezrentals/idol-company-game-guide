PvP — Stats, Upgrades & Battle

Everything that affects your combat power. Sources: Kserske PvP 101 (Aug 2025), Doug Judy Base Stats guide (Feb 2025).

The fundamental rule: Sing = Attack  ·  Dance = Defense  ·  Manage = finances only (zero PvP value). Focus Sing for offense, don’t neglect Dance for defense. Fans = army size — more fans = more firepower in battle.
Upgrade Priority Order

Always improve base numbers before stacking % multipliers on top. Percentages scale off the base — a higher base = bigger multiplier payoff.

#PriorityWhy
1Skills (Photos / Stars)Biggest single stat jump per investment
2Girl LevelRaises base Sing/Dance directly
3Assets & Asset LevelsJewelry first (2★), Cars (3★), Property (4★)
4HQ Glass LevelBuffs ALL girls globally — upgrade before Floor Renovations
5CEO LevelAlso buffs ALL girls globally
6HQ Floor Renovations (Wood/Steel)% multiplier — only after Glass is high
7Car Parts (base number first)Do parts before Car Core — same % logic
8Car Core (Plugs/Coils)% stacked on car base — worthless if base is low
9Blueprints, Collections, Villa, RelicsAll contribute — prioritize after top 3

Rob vs. Rally
TypeHow it worksVerdict & Why
Rob (Solo) You attack alone — only your fans fight Avoid — defender’s harbored fans easily overwhelm a solo attacker
Rally (Group) Group members join your attack — fans combine into one overwhelming force Always use this. Use 5-minute rallies — long rallies lock girls away for hours; 5-minute windows let members join without disrupting their own game.
Artist selection for battle: When two girls are close in level, pick the one with the higher skill star color. Skill buffs apply to your entire fan army — a Yellow-star girl at level 85 beats a Gray-star girl at level 90 in most matchups.
